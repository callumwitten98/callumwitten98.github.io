Research and publications of Callum Witten, including papers on early galaxies, protoclusters and reionisation, with citation metrics updated daily from SciX.

I study galaxies in the first few hundred million years after the Big Bang, using JWST, the VLT and cosmological simulations. Much of my recent work asks how a galaxy's environment, from mergers to dense protoclusters, shaped its growth and its contribution to reionisation.
