research

Highlights from my research on the first galaxies, protoclusters and reionisation.

I study galaxies in the first few hundred million years after the Big Bang, using JWST, the VLT and large-scale cosmological simulations. Much of my recent work establishes how a galaxy's environment, from mergers to dense protoclusters, shaped its growth and its contribution to reionisation.

A few highlights are below. For every paper, along with current bibliometrics, see my publications or my CV.
